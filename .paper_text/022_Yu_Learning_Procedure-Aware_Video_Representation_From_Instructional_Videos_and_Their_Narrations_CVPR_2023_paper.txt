   Once trained, our model offers a procedure-aware rep-
resentation with two key components. First, the video en-
coder f (·) serves as a feature extractor for any input video
clips. Second, the diffusion model, represented as its de-
noising model h(·), captures the temporal dependencies
among steps. Our representation naturally supports a num-
ber of tasks. Here we demonstrate how our model can be
used for step classification and step forecasting.
