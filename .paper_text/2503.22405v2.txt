B.6. Experimental Environment and Hyperparam-
       eters

All experiments are conducted on an Nvidia Tesla V100
GPU with 32GB of VRAM. The training process uses a
batch size of 8 and runs for 200 epochs. The learning rate is
initialized to 0.001 and adjusted dynamically using a cosine
annealing schedule.
